Parent accounts

Kid Mode runs on a guardian's phone. A child never types, and never reaches a purchase.

Plus price

$4.99 a month or $34.99 a year, after a 7-day trial. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Children's data

Nickname, age band, character, accent and the activity record. No photo, birthdate or surname.

The parent gate

A 4-digit PIN leaves Kid Mode. Five wrong tries lock it for 5 minutes.

Routine day

The day starts at 3 in the morning, household time, so a late bedtime still counts as today.

Parent reminders

A guardian opts in. Quiet hours stay 9 at night until 7 in the morning.
