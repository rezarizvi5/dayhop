Privacy

This page is a placeholder for counsel. The product rules below are the ones the app is built to.

A parent provides a child's nickname, age band, character, accent colour and the activity that follows (steps, stars, streaks, reward requests). DayHop does not collect a photo, a birthdate, a surname, a location or anything the child types. Kids have no accounts.

Sign-in is Apple, Google or email, used only to prove the adult's identity. Google scopes are openid and email. Kid Mode sends no analytics. Processors named for the privacy policy are Supabase, RevenueCat, Expo push and Sentry, plus Apple and Google as sign-in providers.

Parents can export household data and delete a kid or the account in the app. A deletion is hidden at once and hard-purged within 7 days. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
